---
title: What I learned writing every week for a year
description: Fifty-two posts later, the habit matters more than any single one.
date: 2026-10-20
tags: [writing, craft]
---

A year ago I promised myself one post a week. I missed three. Here's what the other forty-nine taught me.

1. **Ideas come from writing,** not before it. Sitting down with nothing usually ends with something.
2. **Short is fine.** My most-read post is two hundred words.
3. **Done beats perfect.** Every post I polished for a second week, I liked less.

The archive is the reward: a record of what I was thinking, week by week.
