The tools I reach for every day share a trait: they're small. A text editor that opens instantly. A calculator.
A command that does one thing well.

## Why small wins

Small tools are **easy to understand**, so you trust them. They're **easy to replace**, so you're never stuck.
And they **compose**: two small tools together often beat one big one.

> Make it work, make it right, make it small.

The next time you start building, ask what the smallest useful version is, and ship that first.
