---
title: What our first hundred customers taught us
description: Small businesses don't want more features. They want fewer steps.
date: 2026-09-30
---

We built Acme for accountants. Our first hundred customers were bakers, tailors and design studios.

They didn't ask for reports. They asked for one thing: *make it take less time.* So we cut the invoice form
from nine fields to four, and the time to send one fell from two minutes to twenty seconds.

The lesson we keep relearning: every field is a question someone has to answer.
