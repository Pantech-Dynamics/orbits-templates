---
title: The polite art of chasing a late payment
description: Three reminders, a week apart, get most invoices paid without a single awkward phone call.
date: 2026-10-14
author: Amaka Eze
---

Most late invoices aren't late on purpose. They're sitting in an inbox under forty other emails. A short,
friendly reminder is usually all it takes.

## Before the due date

Send a note three days before: the amount, the date, and the link to pay. People pay what's easy to pay.

## On the day

A second nudge on the due date, shorter than the first. *"Just a reminder that invoice 1042 is due today."*

## A week after

If it's still open, a third message, and this one asks a question: *"Is anything holding this up?"* A question
gets a reply where a reminder gets ignored.

> Keep every message short enough to read on a phone.
